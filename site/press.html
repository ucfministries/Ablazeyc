Official Bio

Long form.

Ablaze Youth Choir is a dynamic, spirit-led movement of young voices committed to igniting a generation for Christ through bold, expressive worship. Rooted in purpose and fueled by passion, Ablaze blends gospel, urban influence, and contemporary sounds to create music that is both culturally relevant and spiritually transformative.

Emerging from United Christian Faith Ministries, Ablaze has captured attention with viral moments, high-energy performances, and a growing digital presence that resonates with youth and families alike. Their sound is playful yet powerful, faith-filled yet relatable, bridging the gap between traditional gospel and today's generation.

More than a choir, Ablaze is a mission. Their music carries a clear message: come to Christ, be encouraged, and live on fire for God. Each song is designed to inspire identity, spark faith, and create authentic worship experiences that extend beyond the stage into everyday life.

With a rapidly expanding audience, a developing fan community, and a vision to reach youth across the globe, Ablaze Youth Choir is not just building songs, they are building a movement that reflects the heart of God and the sound of a generation rising.

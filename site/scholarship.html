Ablaze Scholarship

Investing in
world changers.

The Ablaze Scholarship celebrates students who show commitment, consistency, and spiritual growth as active members of the choir — and supports them as they head into college, trade school, military, or accredited certification programs.

Selection Criteria

How recipients
are chosen.

🎤

Music Ministry

Attendance, vocal contribution, attitude, cooperation, teachability.

✝️

Faith & Character

Maturity in worship, spiritual growth, respect, responsibility, initiative.

🌟

Impact

Contribution to Ablaze culture, ability to encourage others, willingness to serve beyond the stage.

🎯

Future Vision

Clear commitment to continued education or training, purposeful direction.

After The Award

What recipients
commit to.

Ongoing

Stay Connected

Maintain communication with choir leadership and provide periodic updates on education or training progress.

Year One

Represent Excellence

Carry the Ablaze culture into your new environment — whatever school, program, or path you're walking.

Anytime

Come Back Home

Return to support Ablaze for select worship experiences when home or available — special Sundays, homecoming events, youth nights.

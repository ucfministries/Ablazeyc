Questions

Know the
movement.

The quick answers people (and search engines) ask about Ablaze most.

What is Ablaze Youth Choir?

Ablaze Youth Choir (also called Ablaze YC or simply Ablaze) is a spirit-led youth worship movement out of United Christian Faith Ministries in Baton Rouge, Louisiana. It blends gospel, urban influence, and contemporary worship to ignite a generation, online and on stage.

Has Ablaze Youth Choir won any awards?

Yes. Ablaze won New Artist of the Year at the 2026 Stellar Gospel Music Awards, gospel music's biggest night, and was also a 2026 Stellar nominee for Contemporary Choir of the Year. In 2025, Black Christian Influencers named Ablaze Gen Z Influencer of the Year.

Where is Ablaze Youth Choir based?

Ablaze is based in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, and is the youth worship movement of United Christian Faith Ministries. The choir travels and performs nationally.

Who is the director of Ablaze Youth Choir?

Ablaze is directed by Mark Ellis Jr., also known as Marky Mark (@yofavchurchboy), a Gen Z worship leader who fronts the choir on stage and online.

Where can I listen to Ablaze Youth Choir?

You can stream Ablaze on Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, and every major platform. The debut album Ablaze and the latest single Only One are out now.

How can I book Ablaze Youth Choir?

Ablaze takes bookings for churches, conferences, festivals, and youth nights on our booking page. Packages start at $5,000 for a local worship set and scale up to national fly-out experiences.

Is Ablaze Youth Choir part of a church?

Yes. Ablaze is the youth worship movement of United Christian Faith Ministries (UCFM) in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, and worships there every Sunday.

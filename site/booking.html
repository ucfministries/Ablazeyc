Logistics

What we need
from you.

Standard requirements — full details in the contract.

Travel

Worship Set covers in-state. Full Experience covers within 500 miles. National Tour covers full logistics.

Lodging

Overnight events require host-provided lodging based on choir size. Rooming list provided 14 days out.

Hospitality

Day-of meals or per diem. Hospitality rider sent with the agreement.

Sound & Stage

Professionally operated sound system, monitors, stage. Tech rider sent ahead.

Lead Time

Book 60+ days in advance for in-region, 90+ for out-of-state.

Contract

FAQ

Common questions.

How far in advance should we book?

60+ days for events within Louisiana, 90+ days for out-of-state. Best dates fill quickly, especially around conference season.

Do you travel internationally?

Yes, on a case-by-case basis. International bookings are quoted custom under our National Tour tier. Visa lead time and travel logistics typically require 4–6 months notice.

Can we book just Marky Mark for a speaking or solo appearance?

Yes. Mark Ellis Jr., our director, accepts solo bookings — speaking, solo worship, content collabs, and youth conferences.

What's the difference between Full Experience and National Tour?

Full Experience is for single-night premier events within driving distance. National Tour adds multi-day capacity, full production, branded merch, and custom promo.

Are tracks acceptable, or do you require live band?

The Worship Set tier runs with director + tracks. Full Experience and National Tour include our live band.

Do you offer a non-profit or church plant discount?

We work with a limited number of non-profits and church plants each year. Mention it in your inquiry.

What if we need to reschedule?

Reschedules are accommodated when possible. Typically with 30+ days notice we can move dates with no fee.
